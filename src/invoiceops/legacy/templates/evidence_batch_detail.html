How this batch is sealed

  1. Evidence Record datainvoice and evaluation context, model decision, policy, and provenance

  3. Keccak digestone leaf hash per canonical payload
  4. Pair hashes by levelordered pairs form parents until one root remains
  5. Merkle rootthe batch-wide integrity result shown above
